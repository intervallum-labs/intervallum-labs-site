Services

Clear engagements. Steady progress. Real improvements.

Intervallum Labs helps small businesses make everyday work easier to live with — not through one‑size‑fits‑all solutions, but through practical, thoughtful engagements that meet you where you are.

We don’t start with software.

We don’t push big programs or sweeping changes.

We start by understanding how your work actually happens.

Our Structure

How our engagements work

Our services are intentionally structured as engagements, not open‑ended consulting.

Each engagement:

has a clear purpose
a defined scope
and a natural stopping point

That keeps the work focused, affordable, and manageable — for you and for me.

First Engagement

Clarity Session

A calm place to start

Every working relationship starts here.

The Clarity Session is a focused working conversation designed to help you step out of the day‑to‑day noise and get a clear picture of what’s really slowing things down.

This isn’t a sales call or a generic assessment.

It’s a practical, grounded look at how your business actually runs.

What we focus on

  • How work really flows today (not how it “should”)
  • Where manual effort, repetition, or fragile processes show up
  • What’s costing you time, attention, or peace of mind
  • Which problems are worth solving now — and which can wait

What you walk away with

  • A clear understanding of what’s getting in the way
  • A short, prioritized list of meaningful improvements
  • Honest guidance on what makes sense to do next
  • No obligation to continue
Second Engagement

Stabilization Sprint

Making one important thing solid

Once there’s clarity, the next step isn’t to fix everything.

It’s to make one important process calm, reliable, and easier to run.

A Stabilization Sprint is a short, focused engagement where we take a specific workflow or problem area and turn it into something that actually holds up day to day.

What a sprint looks like

  • One clearly defined goal
  • A fixed scope and timeline (usually 2–4 weeks)
  • One meaningful outcome
  • No open‑ended work

Examples of sprint outcomes

  • Replacing spreadsheet‑driven processes
  • Creating reliable intake or follow‑up workflows
  • Reducing inbox‑driven chaos
  • Stabilizing operations that rely on memory or manual steps
  • Making routine work easier to repeat as the business grows

The goal isn’t perfection.

It’s reliability.

Optional

Ongoing Support

Continuity, when it makes sense

Some clients choose to keep working together after a sprint.

Ongoing support is intentionally lightweight and optional. It’s for businesses that want:

  • someone who understands their systems
  • help making small improvements over time
  • continuity without starting from scratch

This is never required and is only discussed once initial work is complete.
