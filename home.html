If your business runs on spreadsheets and duct tape, I get it.

Around here, most small businesses don’t set out to build a mess—it just happens over time. One spreadsheet turns into five. A quick workaround becomes “the way we’ve always done it.” And before you know it, simple tasks take way more effort than they should.

That’s where I come in. Intervallum Labs helps small businesses straighten this out—figuring out what tools actually make sense, setting them up, and making sure they fit the way you run your business.

Why We Exist

Most small businesses don’t need more tools.
They need things to work better.

When work grows a little at a time, the systems behind it usually don’t keep up. Processes get patched together, information lives in too many places, and simple tasks start taking way longer than they should.

Intervallum Labs helps small businesses sort this out. We step in when work has grown organically and help turn it into systems you can actually rely on.

That usually means closing gaps like these:

The tools you’re using today ↔ systems that don’t fall apart
Knowing things could be easier ↔ having a real plan to fix them
Doing everything by hand ↔ work that runs more smoothly day to day

The goal isn’t anything fancy — just fewer frustrations and a business that’s easier to run.

Our Focus

Here’s the kind of stuff we help clean up.

We focus on the little bits of friction that quietly waste time and energy every day, like:

  • Doing the same things over and over by hand
  • Spreadsheets that have turned into “systems” and break easily
  • Tools that don’t talk to each other
  • Important processes that live in someone’s head instead of on paper

Once we understand what’s really going on, we help put better systems in place — the kind that:

  • Cut down on busywork
  • Make things more consistent and easier to follow
  • Hold up as the business grows, without making everything more complicated

The goal is simple: fewer daily headaches and a little breathing room to run the business.

How We Engage

A simple, straightforward way to work together.

We don’t do open‑ended consulting or hourly billing. Most small businesses don’t want a meter running — they want to know what’s happening, why it matters, and what it’s going to cost.

That’s why Intervallum Labs works through clearly defined, fixed‑scope engagements. No surprises, no drifting projects.

Clarity Session

A focused working session where we look at how your business actually runs, figure out what’s getting in the way, and talk through realistic next steps.

You walk away with:

  • A clear understanding of what’s slowing things down
  • A short, prioritized list of improvements that actually make sense
  • A pressure‑free path forward, whether you move ahead or not

Stabilization Sprint

A fixed‑scope engagement to clean up and stabilize one important business process from start to finish.

No big overhauls. No “digital transformation.” Just one meaningful improvement, done properly.

Ongoing Support Optional

Lightweight, clearly bounded support to keep things running smoothly or build on what we’ve already set up — without open‑ended support expectations.
