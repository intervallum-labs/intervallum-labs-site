Pricing

Simple, straightforward pricing

No surprises. No open‑ended work.

Intervallum Labs works through fixed‑scope engagements designed to make progress feel calm and manageable — not complicated or overwhelming. You’ll always know what we’re working on, why it matters, and what it costs.

First Engagement

Clarity Session

$250 One‑time · No obligation

This is where everything starts.

The Clarity Session is a focused working conversation where we step back from the day‑to‑day rush and look at how your business actually runs.

We talk through what’s happening, where things feel heavier than they should, and what’s worth paying attention to right now.

This is not a sales call.

It’s a working session.

What’s included

  • A guided walkthrough of how work really flows today
  • Identifying friction, repetition, and fragile processes
  • A short, prioritized list of opportunities to simplify and stabilize things
  • Honest guidance on what’s worth fixing now — and what isn’t

What you walk away with

  • A clearer picture of what’s getting in the way
  • A shared understanding of what “better” could look like for your business
  • Practical next steps, whether you decide to move forward with us or not
Second Engagement

Stabilization Sprint

Starting at $1,200 Fixed scope · Fixed timeline

Once things are clearer, the next step isn’t to fix everything at once.

It’s to take one important process and make it steady, reliable, and easier to live with.

A Stabilization Sprint is a short, focused engagement where we turn a specific workflow into something that actually holds up day to day.

Examples of what a sprint might include

  • Replacing a spreadsheet‑driven process with something dependable
  • Creating a simple intake‑to‑follow‑up workflow
  • Reducing inbox‑driven work
  • Making internal operations less dependent on memory or manual steps
  • Stabilizing a process that causes daily frustration or rework

How sprints work

  • A clearly defined goal and scope
  • A fixed timeline (typically 2–4 weeks)
  • Clear success criteria agreed on up front
  • No open‑ended “while you’re in there…” work

This keeps projects focused, affordable, and realistic — especially for small businesses.

Optional

Ongoing Support

Monthly · Light‑touch · Only if it makes sense

Some clients choose to keep working together after a sprint.

Ongoing support is intentionally lightweight and optional. It’s meant for businesses that want:

  • someone who already understands their systems
  • help keeping things steady as the business grows
  • small improvements over time without starting from scratch

This is never required and is only discussed after initial work is complete, so expectations stay clear on both sides.

Our Approach

Why pricing works this way

We intentionally avoid:

  • hourly billing
  • “unlimited” support promises
  • large upfront transformation packages

Those models tend to:

  • create anxiety for small businesses
  • blur boundaries
  • burn people out on both sides

Fixed‑scope work keeps things clear, fair, and sustainable.

Our Philosophy

A note on value

We don’t price based on time spent clicking buttons or configuring software.

We price based on:

  • reducing unnecessary human effort
  • removing daily friction from how work gets done
  • creating breathing room to focus on the parts of the business that matter

When work feels calmer and more reliable, growth becomes possible.
